GEO Prompt Tracker

See how often we show up in AI answers for the questions our customers actually ask — across every major model — and get a clear plan to show up more.

Owner: Growth / SEO Status: Proposal Ask: green-light Phase 0
Why now

Search is moving into AI answers

Buyers increasingly ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews instead of scrolling a results page. Those tools name a short list of brands — and if we're not on it, we're invisible, with no clicks to even measure the loss.

Today we have zero visibility into whether AI engines mention us or our competitors, which prompts trigger a mention, or what moves the needle. This tool gives us that visibility and a playbook to act on it.

In one line

What it does

Track our share of voice in AI answers for real buyer prompts, benchmark it against competitors, and recommend exactly what to publish or fix to get mentioned more.

The build

Five building blocks

1Crawl & ingestPaste our site and competitor URLs; crawler pulls pages, topics and entities.
2Keyword miningExtract and cluster keywords and themes by search intent.
3Prompt engineTurn keywords into realistic buyer prompts, ranked by commercial intent.
4Multi-LLM trackerRun prompts on a schedule; detect brand & competitor mentions, citations, position, sentiment.
5Score & recommendShare of voice, citation rate, gaps, and a prioritised action list.

The payoff

How we increase AI mentions

  • Publish answer-first, quotable content — clear claims, stats and definitions LLMs can lift.
  • Earn mentions on sources models trust: Reddit, Wikipedia, YouTube, G2 / Capterra, industry listicles, PR.
  • Build comparison and "best X" pages — the format LLMs reach for when recommending.
  • Add structured data, clear entity naming, and author E-E-A-T signals.
  • Keep facts fresh and consistent across the web so models corroborate them.
  • Grow topical authority with content clusters around our core themes.

Under the hood

Tech approach

  • Crawl: headless browser + sitemap parse
  • Keywords: NLP entity/keyphrase extraction
  • Prompts: LLM generation
  • Tracking: model APIs + mention matching (NER + fuzzy)
  • Stack: Next.js, worker queue, Postgres, scheduled jobs
How we measure

Success metrics

  • Share of voice in AI answers
  • Citation rate & average position
  • Sentiment of mentions
  • Mention trend over time
  • Business: pipeline influenced
Watch-outs

Risks & open questions

  • Compliant ways to query engines at scale (ToS)
  • Cost: prompts × engines × frequency
  • Accuracy of "mention" attribution
  • LLM answers vary — need sampling / averaging
